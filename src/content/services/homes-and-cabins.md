A good house starts with the ground it stands on. Before we draw, we spend a day on the site with you: where the wind comes from, where snow settles, where the sun is at nine in the morning in January, and which view you want from the kitchen table.

From there we work in models, not renderings. Simple massing models let you understand the volume of the house early, while it is still cheap to change. Once the plan feels right, we develop the details that make a building last in a northern climate: deep window reveals, ventilated timber cladding, roofs that shed snow where it does no harm.

Cabins get the same care as primary homes. Many of our clients use their cabin more than they expected, so we design them to work in every season and to be easy to heat, close down and open up again.
