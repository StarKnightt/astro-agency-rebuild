Public buildings are used hard, by many people, for a long time. We design them to be robust and generous: wide stairs that double as seating, daylight in every room people spend time in, and materials that can be cleaned, repaired and replaced without special contractors.

Most of our public work is in timber. Cross-laminated timber and glulam let us build quickly and with a much lower carbon footprint than concrete, and the warm surfaces make a real difference in a library or a classroom in the middle of winter.

We work closely with the client organisation through the whole process. The best decisions in our projects have often come from a caretaker, a librarian or a teacher who knows exactly how a building is used at eight in the morning.
